Serving our community since 1992

Jung Ko Taekwondo has been part of the GTA and surrounding communities as a first-class provider in the education and sport of taekwondo.

Our program blends the traditional martial art with modern anti-bullying, self-defence and confidence-building techniques. It's why parents have trusted us with their children for more than three decades.

The school is led by Grandmaster Jung Ko, a 9th Dan Kukkiwon certificate holder and a recipient of the Queen Elizabeth II Golden Jubilee Medal for his outstanding contributions to the community.

    How the free trial works

    1. Send a trial request

      Choose an age group and the days that suit you. We'll call to confirm a time.

    2. Come to the dojang

      Unit 200, 180 Steeles Ave W, Thornhill, with on-site parking. Parents can watch from the viewing area.

    3. Get a recommendation

      After class, the instructor will suggest the right program for your child.

    Common areas are under 24-hour video surveillance. Our team includes first aid and CPR certified instructors.

    Questions parents ask

    Is my 3-year-old ready?

    The trial class is how we find out. Some children are ready right away, and some need more than one trial before they join.

    Can I watch the class?

    Yes. There's a full parent viewing area beside the training floor.

    Do you pick kids up from school?

    Yes, through our after-school program. We pick up from 11 local schools, including Brownridge, Steelesview, Yorkhill and Rockford. Call us if your school isn't listed.

    How does my child join the competitive or demo team?

    Both teams are by invitation. Students who are interested should speak with Grandmaster Ko.

    Is sparring safe?

    Sparring (kyorugi) is taught as a sport, not a fighting technique.

    Where do I buy a uniform?

    In our online shop. Uniform sets are $65, and we also carry sparring gear and targets.
